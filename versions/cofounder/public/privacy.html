Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026 · Co-founder scope updated October 17, 2026

evolution ("we", "us") operates runevolution.io and the evolution service (the "Service"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email and authentication details when sign-in is configured.
  • Workspace content: the product idea, plan, messages and outputs you choose to enter or generate.
  • Technical data: hosting and service logs used to provide and secure the application.
  • Optional voice: speech input is handled by your browser’s speech-recognition service after you choose to use it and grant permission. Browser voice services may process audio remotely.

2. How we use it

To provide your co-founder conversations, prepare requested outputs, authenticate your account, provide support and maintain the service. Your message, relevant conversation history and company context are sent to the configured AI provider to generate a reply. This workspace does not collect payment details or connect your store.

3. How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers / subprocessors who help us run the Service (for example hosting, analytics, payment processing, and AI/model providers), under confidentiality obligations and only as needed.
  • Legal reasons, if required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers, if we are involved in a merger or acquisition.

4. Workspace storage and your choices

Your co-founder idea, approved plan and role conversations are saved in local browser storage, separately from the ecommerce application and under your signed-in account identifier. They do not automatically sync to another device. Use Download work to keep a copy. Clearing this browser’s site data removes its saved workspace and may also sign you out. AI requests and service logs are processed by the relevant providers under their own retention practices; local deletion does not itself delete provider records.

5. Connected accounts

The AI co-founder workspace does not request access to Shopify, repositories, email inboxes or social accounts. Do not enter passwords, API keys or login codes in chat. The preserved ecommerce service has separate connections and records; contact us for questions or deletion requests relating to an existing ecommerce account.

6. Subprocessors

  • Vercel: application hosting.
  • Supabase: account authentication when configured.
  • Groq: the current server-side AI generation provider. Submitted messages and relevant workspace context are sent for inference.
  • Your browser speech provider: optional voice recognition and speech playback, depending on browser support and your choices.

7. Data retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, though no method is 100% secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to certain processing. Email subhan@runevolution.io or muneeb@runevolution.io to make a request.

9. International users

We may process data in countries other than yours. By using the Service you consent to this, subject to applicable law.

10. Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new effective date. Significant changes will be communicated where required.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: subhan@runevolution.io or muneeb@runevolution.io.
