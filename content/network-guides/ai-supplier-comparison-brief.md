title: How to request a sourced supplier comparison from an AI agent
description: Compare supplier information with consistent units, dated sources and explicit unknowns, without letting an agent make purchasing decisions.

A supplier comparison is only helpful when the rows mean the same thing. An attractive table can hide different pack sizes, outdated prices or shipping terms that were never confirmed. An AI agent can organize the evidence, but the brief needs to define what can and cannot be compared.

Start with a limited research task. Gathering public information or organizing documents is different from contacting suppliers, accepting terms or placing an order. Keep those actions outside the initial scope.

## Define the decision without delegating it

List the criteria that matter to your business: product specification, minimum order quantity, lead time, shipping destination and available documentation. Decide which are mandatory and which are simply useful context.

Do not ask for the best supplier without defining best. Instead, request a table of evidence and a separate list of unanswered questions. The final selection can remain with the person responsible for purchasing.

## Normalize units without hiding originals

An illustrative comparison might show original pack size, quoted pack price and a calculated unit price in separate columns. Record the currency and calculation rule. If comparable shipping costs are unavailable, do not describe the cheapest listed product as the cheapest delivered option.

Avoid silently converting an estimated delivery period into a promise. Preserve the source wording and note whether the information is a public statement or a current quote addressed to your business.

## Require a source for each important claim

Ask for a URL or document reference and the date checked. If information conflicts, retain both references. A blank field labeled not found is more useful than a plausible number inferred from another supplier.

Only share private supplier documents when you have permission and an appropriate handling arrangement. A public request can describe the comparison without publishing contracts, account numbers or confidential price lists.

## Make the trial easy to audit

Use a small set of suppliers and inspect a few fields yourself. Include one case with missing information and one where the units differ. Check that the agent flags the mismatch instead of ranking incompatible offers together.

Require a final questions-to-confirm section. It might identify a missing shipping quote or an unclear minimum order quantity, rather than claiming the research settled every issue. This converts uncertainty into a practical next step.

If the result is useful, define how often it should be refreshed and which changes deserve attention. Refreshing a table should not automatically authorize supplier outreach or purchasing.
