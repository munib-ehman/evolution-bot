Frequently asked questions

Everything founders usually ask us before their free audit.

What is Evolution?

Evolution is software — one AI operator that runs your Shopify store's busywork automatically: cart recovery, customer win-backs, and fulfillment tracking. It's connected to your real store data, and every action waits for your approval.

Does a human do the work?

No. Evolution is software — AI agents do the work automatically. During onboarding we may help you connect a password-protected store, but the product itself runs on its own.

Do I stay in control?

Yes. Every action requires your approval before the software runs it. Nothing sends or changes without your yes.

How does the free audit work?

Connect your store and the software automatically audits it across six areas, showing where you're losing sales ranked by dollar impact, plus three winning product ideas for your niche. Instant, no credit card required. Request yours here.

What does it cost?

The automated audit is free. The Operator plan — automated cart recovery, customer retention, and the fulfillment ops dashboard — is $100/month, billed monthly, cancel anytime, with a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment. See pricing for details.

What do I get when I pay?

Instant access to your operator dashboard, with the automations ready to switch on. No waiting, no "we'll get started" — you're in the app immediately.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in one click. Plus a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment — the details are in our refund policy.

Is my store data safe?

Evolution connects with read-only Shopify access to start, uses named subprocessors, and follows data-minimization. See our privacy policy.
