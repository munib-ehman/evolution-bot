Frequently asked questions

Everything founders usually ask us before their free audit.

What is Evolution?

Evolution is an AI business operator — an AI COO — for ecommerce store owners. It handles the day-to-day busywork you'd otherwise split across a VA and a stack of single-purpose apps, in one accountable tool.

What does it actually handle?

  • Abandoned cart and checkout recovery (email + SMS)
  • Customer support and tickets — where-is-my-order, returns, replies
  • Product listings, descriptions, and SEO
  • Reviews and social-proof collection
  • Email/SMS lifecycle flows and retention
  • Ads and creative support
  • Analytics and reporting — true profit, what's actually working

How does the free audit work?

You share your store URL, and Evolution audits the whole store: checkout flow, product pages, emails, reviews, support, and SEO. You get every leak we find, ranked by dollar impact, plus a plan for what to fix first. No credit card required. Request yours here.

Who is it for?

Independent Shopify and DTC founders doing roughly $50k to $5M a year — the stage where you're stretched thin running everything yourself, or paying a VA plus several apps to keep up.

What does it cost?

The audit and a limited number of tasks are free. The Operator plan — the AI COO running your busywork end to end — is $100/month, billed monthly, cancel anytime. That's about a tenth of what the usual app stack costs. See pricing for details.

What does "you pay only if it works" mean?

Start with the free audit and free tasks — you see the value before you pay anything. If you're on the Operator plan and we fail to complete the work we agreed to do for your store, we'll make it right. The details are in our refund policy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Requesting the audit is just sharing your store URL. The app itself runs in your browser — nothing to install on your store or your computer.

Is my data safe?

We only collect what we need to run the service, and we spell it out in plain language in our privacy policy.

How do I get started?

Request your free audit — it takes two minutes. Already have an account? Sign in.
