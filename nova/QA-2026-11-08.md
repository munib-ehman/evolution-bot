# Nova v4 verification — November 8, 2026 (Asia/Karachi)

## Outcome

110 local backend tests pass. External providers are simulated. Python compilation and git diff checks pass. A separate interactive product preview is published at https://nova-evolution-v4.vercel.app. The production marketplace at runevolution.io was not replaced.

The preview is a scripted walkthrough, not an AI-connected operator. It provides reviewable onboarding, sample business knowledge, workflow review/activation, message approval/rejection, pause/resume, activity reports and a guarded signup screen. No real details are submitted, payments collected, or messages sent. Its scripts do not call external services.

## Fixed failures

- Scheduled instructions could change owner permissions. Permission commands now require an owner/call event.
- Voice-message opt-outs were checked before transcription. Transcription now precedes opt-out handling and opted-out contacts remain suppressed.
- Quote recipe activation did not start quote preparation. Approved lead capture now queues a scoped quote task; proposals are limited to that lead and the quote tool.
- A checkout still open after one hour could produce another session. It is now reused regardless of age. Unknown statuses and old ambiguous attempts require reconciliation.
- Scheduled work omitted the WhatsApp channel, losing approval/result notices. Scheduled events now retain it, including older pending events.
- Approved outbound calls arrived with Nova as From and were rejected as unknown owners. Callbacks now resolve the approved action, validate owner destination and provider call ID, and preserve their binding through speech turns.
